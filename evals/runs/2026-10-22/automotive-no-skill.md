# Research baseline

- Run date: 2026-10-22
- Condition: No Skill
- Exact prompt: “Research China’s new-energy vehicle value-chain profit pools for 2023–2025. Select representative companies with different roles; explain where revenue and profit mainly accrue and why; then propose credible external startup entry points and classify AI in each as core capability, important enhancement, ordinary feature, or unnecessary. Clearly distinguish facts, calculations, and inference, and state data gaps.”

## Answer

### Scope and headline

This is a China-centred NEV value-chain view for FY2023–FY2025. “Profit pool” means the economic profit captured by a role, not revenue or enterprise value. The three representatives below are deliberately different: Ganfeng Lithium (lithium resource/refining), CATL (battery systems), and BYD (vertically integrated NEV OEM). Their reported figures are consolidated-company figures, not pure China-NEV segment profits; that distinction is material.

**Fact.** China’s NEV sales rose from 9.50m in 2023 to 12.87m in 2024 and 16.49m in 2025 (the latter +28.2% YoY and 47.9% of new-vehicle sales). The Ministry of Industry and Information Technology reports the 2025 total and has stated the 2024 total in its policy briefing. [MIIT, 2025 outcome](https://www.miit.gov.cn/xwfb/xwfbh/bxwfbh/art/2026/art_c5c9031e409e468eb4004d82fd394241.html); [MIIT, 2024 outcome](https://wap.miit.gov.cn/jgsj/zbys/qcgy/art/2025/art_7ab108aaf199462f8da54fdecfa527e1.html). The 2023 figure is CAAM’s published annual result (9.495m); it is included for trend context.

**Inference.** Volume growth made the chain much larger, but did not make every layer more profitable. The evidence below is consistent with bargaining power and product differentiation moving to scaled battery-system suppliers, while commodity lithium was price-sensitive and domestic vehicle competition constrained OEM capture.

All currency figures are RMB billions, rounded to one decimal. “Parent profit” is profit attributable to owners of the parent, to keep the three companies comparable.

**Calculations.** Parent-profit margin = parent profit ÷ consolidated revenue. On this consistent, deliberately simple basis: CATL was **11.0%, 14.0%, 17.0%** in 2023–25; BYD was **5.0%, 5.2%, 4.1%**; Ganfeng was **15.0%, -11.0%, 7.0%**. Rounding can cause a 0.1pp difference. These are not gross margins, segment margins, cash returns, or estimates of a supplier’s China-only NEV margin.

### Where revenue and profit accrue

1. **Revenue principally accrues at the finished vehicle and battery-system layers.** This is visible in scale: BYD reported RMB804.0bn of consolidated FY2025 revenue and CATL RMB423.7bn, compared with Ganfeng’s RMB23.1bn. The vehicle layer sells the highest-ticket finished product; the battery layer monetises a central, technically specified subsystem across many OEM programs. This comparison must not be added together: BYD buys/produces batteries and each company also has non-NEV and overseas activity.

2. **The clearest recurring profit pool in this sample is scaled battery technology and manufacturing.** **Fact:** CATL’s calculated attributable margin rose from 11.0% to 17.0% while revenue first fell in 2024 and recovered in 2025. It spent RMB22.1bn on R&D in 2025, sold 661 GWh, and had 772 GWh production capacity at year-end. [CATL 2025 release](https://www.catl.com/en/news/6773.html) **Inference:** qualification cycles, safety/performance responsibility, manufacturing yield, scale purchasing and OEM switching costs give leading cell/pack suppliers more ability to defend economics than a commodity seller. This is not proof that every battery maker earns CATL-like returns.

3. **Upstream mining/refining is an important but cyclical profit pool, not a stable one.** **Fact:** Ganfeng’s parent profit changed from +RMB4.9bn in 2023 to -RMB2.1bn in 2024 and +RMB1.6bn in 2025; its FY2025 report calls out a substantial gross-profit increase and gains on disposals/investments in the recovery. [Ganfeng 2025 annual report](https://www1.hkexnews.hk/listedco/listconews/sehk/2026/0430/2026043002397_c.pdf) **Inference:** resource ownership can capture scarcity rents when lithium prices are tight, but oversupply/price declines, inventory/asset effects and high fixed costs can rapidly erase them. The 2025 rebound should therefore not be read as a return to a structurally high resource margin.

4. **OEMs retain the largest revenue pool but face the sharpest price/feature competition.** **Fact:** BYD’s revenue increased RMB26.9bn in 2025 while parent profit fell RMB7.6bn; calculated margin fell about 1.1 percentage points. **Inference:** vehicle brands can capture system-integration, software, distribution and financing value, but dense domestic model competition passes much of productivity and component-cost improvement to buyers. Vertically integrated OEMs can internalise some battery value, so a standalone battery-to-OEM comparison is inherently imperfect.

5. **End-of-life is becoming a regulated, strategic pool rather than a mature free-profit pool.** **Fact:** China had more than **400,000 tonnes** of spent NEV power-battery comprehensive utilisation in 2025, +32.9%, and the ministry expects more than 1m tonnes of annual retired batteries by 2030. It also describes 148 recognised comprehensive-utilisation backbone enterprises and a national traceability system/digital battery identity. [MIIT briefing](https://www.miit.gov.cn/xwfb/xwfbh/bxwfbh/art/2026/art_c5c9031e409e468eb4004d82fd394241.html) **Inference:** the likely value is in compliant collection, verified state-of-health, safe logistics and feedstock routing; not simply owning a generic dismantling plant.

### Credible external startup entry points

The opportunities below intentionally avoid “build a new cell factory,” “be another mass-market OEM,” and unlicensed collection/refining. Those require capital, qualification, channel rights and safety/compliance infrastructure that a normal external startup does not have.

| Entry point | Customer and monetisation | Why it is credible now | AI classification | What must be true |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Battery passport / compliant reverse-logistics operating system** | OEMs, battery makers, dealers, fleets and approved recyclers; per-pack traceability, routing and compliance SaaS plus transaction/service fees. | Producers must provide collection networks and transfer batteries into compliant channels; policy is moving to full-lifecycle traceability and a battery digital identity. [MIIT responsibilities](https://www.miit.gov.cn/xwfb/xwfbh/bxwfbh/art/2026/art_c5c9031e409e468eb4004d82fd394241.html) | **Important enhancement.** AI can extract documents, flag anomalous flows and optimise routing; the core asset is trusted integrations, auditability and local operating execution. | Secure OEM/recycler partnerships and data permissions; do not position it as a substitute for the state platform or as a recycler without required qualifications. |
| **Independent fleet battery health, residual-value and warranty-risk service** | Leasing firms, logistics fleets, insurers and used-NEV marketplaces; recurring monitoring plus underwriting/inspection fees. | Battery condition directly changes resale and warranty exposure, while fleets produce repeatable telemetry and maintenance records. Growth in the installed base makes the problem more valuable. | **Core capability.** The product’s differentiated output is a calibrated degradation/failure/residual-value model built from pack telemetry, charging, climate and repair outcomes. | Start with one chemistry/platform and one fleet partner; validate predictions against observed capacity tests and claims before pricing insurance or guarantees. |
| **Recycling intake triage and safe disassembly decision support** | Licensed recyclers and dismantlers; per-line software/vision subscription plus integration services. | Regulation focuses on safe, compliant hand-off; MIIT highlights automation, efficient metal extraction and high-value utilisation, while feedstock is scaling. | **Important enhancement.** Vision/ML can identify pack type/damage and recommend handling/routing. Safety procedures, certified equipment, chemistry knowledge and licensed execution remain the core. | Sell into authorised operators; retain human safety authority and prove low false-negative rates for damaged/high-voltage packs. |
| **Commercial-fleet charging and depot energy optimisation** | Bus/logistics fleets, charging operators and site owners; share of energy savings or SaaS per vehicle/site. | NEV scale raises simultaneous charging-load problems; batteries, storage and charging are increasingly coupled. | **Important enhancement.** Forecasting, optimisation and anomaly detection improve dispatch, but contracted electricity, site interconnection, charging hardware uptime and customer operations determine whether value is captured. | Target constrained depots with measurable tariff/demand-charge pain; do not claim grid services without the necessary market and utility permissions. |
| **Generic AI car-shopping chatbot or “AI dashboard” widget** | Consumer apps/OEM dealers; advertising or software fee. | Easy to prototype but little protected access to inventory, finance, aftersales or vehicle-control workflows. | **Ordinary feature.** It may improve conversion/support but is not a defensible entry point by itself. | Only pursue as an add-on to owned transaction, service or fleet workflow data. |

### Data gaps and limits

- Neither BYD nor Ganfeng’s consolidated revenue/profit is a clean China-NEV figure; both have other businesses, geographies, investments and intra-group integration. CATL also has energy storage and overseas sales. This prevents a valid “profit share of the China NEV chain” calculation from these filings alone.
- Public disclosures do not provide a consistent 2023–25 map of OEM battery procurement price, cell chemistry mix, supplier gross margin, dealer/finance margin, warranty cost, charging utilisation, recycling collection cost, or economic profit after cost of capital. Those are the variables needed to size each pool rigorously.
- Lithium price, inventory write-down, impairment, fair-value and disposal effects mean Ganfeng’s net profit is not a clean realised refining margin. BYD’s 2023 comparatives are labelled restated in its FY2025 report.
- NEV sales are industry wholesale statistics, not retail registrations; they include exports in the official CAAM framing. They are a demand-scale indicator, not an exact China domestic consumer-revenue denominator.
- The startup recommendations are **inferences**, not market-size forecasts or investment advice. A next research pass should obtain OEM/fleet/recycler interviews, pack-level telemetry and claims data, provincial licensing/channel economics, and segmented financials before assigning TAM, pricing or a probability of success.
