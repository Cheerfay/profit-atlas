# Sector Question Bank

Use the common workflow in SKILL.md, then add only the relevant sector questions.

## Securities

### Revenue and profit

- What share comes from brokerage commissions, investment banking, asset management, net interest income, proprietary trading, market making, and principal investment?
- Which lines are fee-based, spread-based, balance-sheet-based, or market-direction-dependent?
- How much profit comes from trading gains rather than recurring client revenue?
- What is the revenue and profit contribution of wealth management versus traditional brokerage?
- Are investment-banking fees concentrated in IPOs, bonds, refinancing, or advisory?

### Costs and balance sheet

- What are compensation and benefit costs as a percentage of revenue?
- How large are technology, branch, clearing, exchange, and regulatory costs?
- What is the leverage ratio, net capital, liquidity coverage, and regulatory capital position?
- How much funding comes from client deposits, margin financing, repurchase agreements, bonds, and bank loans?
- What is the credit risk in margin lending, stock pledges, derivatives, and proprietary positions?

### Opportunity test

- Does the startup need broker-dealer licenses or access to client transactions?
- Can it sell to multiple brokers as neutral infrastructure?
- Is the real buyer compliance, research, wealth management, operations, or IT?
- Does the product reduce regulatory risk, adviser labor, settlement error, or capital usage?

## Insurance

### Revenue and profit

- Separate premiums, insurance service revenue, investment income, fee income, and reinsurance effects.
- For property and casualty insurance, inspect loss ratio, expense ratio, and combined ratio.
- For life insurance, inspect new-business value, contractual service margin, surrender behavior, product mix, and investment spread.
- Which products generate underwriting profit, and which rely on investment returns?
- How much profit comes from reserve assumption changes or one-off investment gains?

### Costs and balance sheet

- Claims, commissions, acquisition cost, operating expenses, reinsurance cost, and reserve development.
- Solvency ratio, asset-liability duration mismatch, credit exposure, and liquidity.
- Distribution dependence on agents, banks, brokers, platforms, and direct channels.

### Opportunity test

- Is the opportunity inside pricing, underwriting, claims, fraud, distribution, policy servicing, or asset-liability management?
- Does it require regulated decision authority or only decision support?
- Can a vendor obtain claims and policy data across carriers?
- Does the solution improve loss ratio, expense ratio, conversion, persistency, or capital efficiency?

## Real Estate

### Revenue and profit

- Separate development sales, rental income, property management, commercial operations, construction, and asset management.
- Distinguish contracted sales from recognized revenue and cash collection.
- Inspect gross margin after land and construction cost, impairment, fair-value gains, and disposal gains.
- Which projects, cities, or property types generate profit?

### Costs and balance sheet

- Land bank, inventory under development, completed inventory, receivables, pre-sale contract liabilities, restricted cash, and guarantees.
- Short- and long-term debt, average funding cost, maturity schedule, and secured assets.
- Net gearing, cash-to-short-term-debt ratio, interest coverage, and operating cash flow.
- Separate accounting profit from cash available to the parent company.

### Opportunity test

- Does the opportunity depend on developer access, government land data, property ownership, brokerage traffic, or local service teams?
- Is the payer the developer, owner, tenant, property manager, lender, or municipality?
- Can the product work across projects and cities, or must it be rebuilt locally?
- Does it reduce vacancy, construction cost, energy use, sales cycle, financing risk, or maintenance cost?

## Games

### Revenue and profit

- Separate mobile, PC, console, advertising, licensing, publishing, and merchandise.
- Split by game, region, platform, and self-developed versus licensed titles when disclosed.
- Compare bookings, recognized revenue, deferred revenue, and active users.
- Identify evergreen titles, new-title launches, and hit dependence.
- Estimate platform fees, channel share, developer revenue share, content amortization, and server cost.

### Costs

- R&D and studio payroll.
- User acquisition and marketing payback.
- Platform commissions and publishing revenue share.
- Content impairment and capitalization policy.
- Live-operations, customer support, anti-cheat, moderation, and cloud costs.

### Opportunity test

- Does the tool improve content production, live operations, monetization, localization, moderation, testing, or user acquisition?
- Can a platform or engine bundle the feature?
- Is proprietary game data needed, and can a vendor obtain it?
- Is willingness to pay tied to revenue lift or labor reduction?

## E-commerce

### Revenue and profit

- Separate first-party retail, third-party commissions, advertising, logistics, membership, payments, and cloud or merchant services.
- Track GMV, revenue, take rate, active buyers, merchants, order frequency, and average order value.
- Distinguish gross revenue from net commission revenue.
- Identify which businesses generate gross profit and which create traffic or fulfillment density.

### Costs and cash flow

- Product cost, fulfillment, delivery, payment processing, traffic acquisition, subsidies, returns, fraud, and customer service.
- Inventory ownership and write-down risk.
- Merchant deposits, customer prepayments, payables, receivables, and restricted funds.
- Explain negative working capital and whether it persists when growth slows.

### Opportunity test

- Does the startup depend on marketplace traffic or merchant transaction data?
- Is the buyer the platform, brand, merchant, logistics provider, or consumer?
- Can the platform copy and bundle the feature?
- Does the product improve conversion, ad return, inventory turns, fulfillment cost, return rate, or merchant compliance?

## Local Services

### Revenue and profit

- Separate commissions, advertising, delivery fees, membership, payment services, and first-party operations.
- Track GTV or GMV, orders, take rate, active consumers, merchants, riders, and cities.
- Split in-store services from delivery and travel or ticketing when relevant.
- Identify whether profit comes from advertising, commission, logistics, or financial services.

### Costs and network economics

- Rider and courier cost, subsidies, merchant incentives, consumer coupons, payment fees, insurance, customer service, and city operations.
- Order density, delivery distance, utilization, peak-hour imbalance, and city maturity.
- Separate mature-city contribution margin from expansion-city losses.

### Opportunity test

- Does the opportunity require a local supply network or only software?
- Can it serve merchants across platforms without violating platform rules?
- Is the buyer a platform, chain merchant, independent merchant, logistics fleet, or property operator?
- Does it reduce delivery cost, idle capacity, food waste, labor scheduling, marketing waste, or compliance burden?
- Will the platform bundle it once proven?
