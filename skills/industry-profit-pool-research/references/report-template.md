# Report Template

Adapt the depth to the user's question, but preserve the logic below.

## 1. Scope and source note

- Company or industry.
- Fiscal years.
- Currency and unit.
- Audited versus unaudited data.
- Primary filings used.

## 2. Executive judgment

State three to five conclusions:

- Core revenue source.
- Core gross-profit or operating-profit source.
- Main cost or balance-sheet risk.
- Key change across the period.
- External startup conclusion.

## 3. Three-year financial overview

| Operating cash flow | | | |

## 4. Revenue and profit sources

For each disclosed segment:

| Segment | Revenue | Cost | Gross profit | Gross margin | Evidence type |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|

Then identify:

- Main profit engine.
- High-margin but small business.
- Strategic loss leader.
- One-off or project-based contributor.
- Non-operating contributor.

## 5. Cost structure

Cover:

- Direct cost and unit economics.
- R&D.
- Sales and marketing or distribution.
- General and administrative costs.
- Fulfillment, claims, content, commissions, interest, or other sector-specific costs.
- Fixed versus variable cost behavior.

## 6. Balance sheet and cash flow

Explain:

- Liquidity and net cash/debt.
- Supplier, customer, policyholder, tenant, or platform financing.
- Inventory, receivables, deferred revenue, reserves, and commitments.
- Why operating cash flow differs from profit.

## 7. Why performance changed

Use the most relevant drivers:

- Volume.
- Price and mix.
- Cost reduction.
- Product cycle.
- Utilization.
- Competition.
- Regulation.
- Working capital.
- One-off accounting items.

## 8. Industry profit map

| Value-chain role | Revenue model | Typical economics | Control point | Entry barrier |
|---|---|---|---|---|

Separate market size, revenue pool, and profit pool.

## 9. External startup screen

| Opportunity | Buyer and budget | Why external | Integration burden | Defensibility | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Be willing to conclude that no attractive external wedge is visible.

## 10. Data gaps and page index

List:

- Important undisclosed segment costs or margins.
- Estimates and formulas.
- PDF viewer pages and printed pages for the load-bearing evidence.
